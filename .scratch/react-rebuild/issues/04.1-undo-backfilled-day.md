# 04.1: Undo a back-filled habit day

**Parent:** 04

**What to build:** Make removal symmetric with back-fill. Today `POST /habits/{id}/unlog/` only reverses a *today* log: it decrements today's daily count and withdraws a streak credit where `last_completed_date == today`. A mistaken back-fill (R23c) is not reversible — its weekly/monthly bumps can only be undone by an unlog that also (wrongly) decrements today's daily count, and a streak credit it granted for a past day cannot be withdrawn at all. The same hole exists for a log made just before local midnight and undone just after.

Likely shape: `unlog` accepts an optional `date` (mirroring `log`), reverses the weekly/monthly bumps when the date is in the current week/month, and withdraws a streak credit where `last_completed_date == date`. Note the no-history limits: a backwards streak extension (day before the streak started) cannot be selectively withdrawn, and per-day counts are unknown — document whatever approximation is chosen.
