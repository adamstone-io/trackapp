# 06.10: A studied row leaves the list, and retired items fold away

**Parent:** 06 (Study page core)

Two things 06.9 broke or exposed, reported on the live page.

**A studied row stayed put.** Pressing Study then Done collapsed the answer and
left the row exactly where it was. The old list re-sorted itself in the browser
on every render, so logging an interaction made the row the most recently
touched item there was and it jumped to the bottom of ~2,300 — gone from view.
Server-side ordering does not reposition anything, and the optimistic patch
only touched the counts and timestamps.

**Retired items sat at the foot of the list.** They always had, but under
`fetchAllPages` they were buried below thousands of rows and nobody ever
scrolled that far. Loading twenty at a time put them immediately under the
first screenful.

**Blocked by:** 06.9 — done

**Status:** done (2026-09-20)

- [x] Logging a prime or a study moves the row to the back of the queue: to the
      end of the list when the last page is loaded, out of view when it is not
      (both are where R31w puts it)
- [x] The row's own count stays honest — it is still in the collection, so the
      collection size does not move
- [x] Retired items are folded behind an "Archived study items (n)" toggle,
      collapsed on arrival, so restoring one (R29) stays reachable without the
      list being in the way
- [x] `PaginatedPage` carries `count`, and optimistic insertions and removals
      adjust it, or the toggle's number lags a refetch
- [x] Tests: a studied row leaves a partly-loaded list; it moves to the bottom
      of a fully-loaded one; the archived section is folded on arrival

**Not a new requirement:** the re-queue follows from R31w — a logged
interaction is the item's most recent touch, and the list is ordered by oldest
touch first. Folding the archived section is a UI decision, not a requirement;
R29 only asks that retiring and restoring are possible.
